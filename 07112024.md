# JULY 11TH, 2024 - Programming Progress So Far

Heya, my name is Mark Hanson, a programmer from Placebo Entertainment and I would like to share the programming team's current progress on the project. Our workflow for how we approach programming tasks have changed a lot to furthermore reflect the image of what the design team wants. With that we have been working even closer than ever to flesh out certain things from minigames, dialogue, certain assets and more. 

Going from alpha to beta we used a new way of pull requesting and reviewing which I believe made our journey even more smoother than the last milestone. We started incorporating designers to review certain fixes and functions earlier in the run making most issues or misunderstandings of intentions cleared up overall improving how many bugs are at the end of the milestone which gave us more time to focus on tasks that actually mattered to get a complete build. I would say after reviewing our later on and pre existing functions that have been fixed, the alpha build version of the game is not only completable, it was fun with all the things we were able to add. Overall I felt with the alpha everyone was in sync amazingly well.

With our new milestone underway I would say we are at a good point in our development. Most of the new changes we have to make are to preexisting things and tons of UI to make the game easy to play even for a 10 year old. I can’t wait to see what this game will look like with all the 3d assets on top of that.

-Mark
