# JUNE 14TH, 2024 - First Playable!

First playable is done! 

It’s always so much fun as a producer to get to look at a build like this and see all the small things people have been working on begin to come together. It was a hard road to get here, that I am sure we will feel going into Alpha, but I am excited either way. It is especially nice because we have a lot of people working remotely this summer. This is great because it means we have people on the team who otherwise wouldn’t have been able to create with us, but it also gives everyone more flexibility (it is our summer break after all). The only problem is it can be really hard to see the progress other people are making when all you get are screenshots in the Discord.  We try to do what we can to show off work while it is getting done, but it doesn’t compare to being all in the same room. 

I do think a fair amount of this disconnect is because the majority of the work I have done for games has been in a classroom environment, which means not only is your entire team in the same room, but often other teams as well. It sounds like a lot, and that’s because it is, but you get used to having constant noise and movement in your workspace that it feels weird to be without it. You also get used to people throwing their work up on the big screen in the room whenever they want to show something off or illustrate a point. There are also benefits to this remote stuff though.

It’s easier for ideas to get saved in a recorded meeting or a chat log, which is a real lifesaver when someone says something cool and then the conversation moves on before anyone can write it down. I also appreciate the fact that I have a quiet place to work, I could never do any kind of email or blog writing in a classroom. And back to my original point, being separated makes seeing everything come together feel so much cooler! 

I’m so proud of my team and their progress, and can’t wait to start Alpha!

-Rachel
