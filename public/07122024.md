# JULY 12TH, 2024 - First Week of Beta

It is so crazy that we are already half way through this project. This summer idea has been something I have been tossing around in mind since 2023, and there was a real part of me that didn’t think it would happen. 

It can be really easy to become discouraged as a college student going into an incredibly competitive industry, especially with where the games industry is right now. I know multiple people who applied for internships that got canceled before interviews had even started, or got to the interview stage only to be ghosted. I was really lucky that I got to work at a company last year, and was even offered a job, but then the studio got shut down. I know that I often worry about how I’m going to fare after graduation, when I’m up against seasoned industry veterans for limited spots. But I didn’t want that worry to define how I spent my days before my senior year of college. I instead want to recenter the stuff that makes me excited to enter the industry. For me that’s working with people on something they care about.

I made this company for a lot of reasons: to learn and grow as a producer and give others the chance to do the same; to prepare for senior capstone; to create a game that people can be proud to put on their portfolios, but most importantly I wanted to remind myself and my team that we can do this. Game design work is hard, entering the industry as a new adult is terrifying, but this project is a way to remember why we want to. I don’t think anyone who wasn’t deeply passionate and incredibly committed to this work would willingly give up their summer evenings week after week. Most of us have full time summer jobs, some with really weird hours, but despite working that 9-5 we come in and pour our hearts into this project. And week after week I hear how excited people are to be doing that work, how much they love getting to collaborate with a team and create things, how satisfying it is to take an idea and bring it to life. This is why we do the work.

I’m not going to pretend a three month summer project fixes all the problems facing a fresh faced college grad. There’s still a ton of competition, and a lot of learning to do. Despite all that, I know all my teammates and I will take everything we’ve learnt here, and all the ways we’ve grown, and apply that to every application we submit, every linkedin message we send, and every interview we go to. I know this post is incredibly sappy, but I can’t help but be amazed at all the work and passion I have seen from this team. Nothing makes me more excited when looking out at the road ahead.

-Rachel
