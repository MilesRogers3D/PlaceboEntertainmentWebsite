# JUNE 21ST, 2024 - Story and World Building

Hi there, my name is Hayden Huber! I’m the lead UX designer here at Placebo Entertainment, however today I’m here to talk a little about what my other task at Placebo has been: story and world! For the past month or so, I have been the lead writer on the game's story, world, and characters. It’s been my task to create the game’s world bible, character backstories, histories and societies, and so on; which is what I want to share here with you today! Though only a little, no major spoilers ;).

The first thing I did was characters. As our cast are the cornerstone of the experience it was important to nail them. Being a fellow member of the design team, I volunteered to create the concepts for characters, including their description, home world, society, and so on. Take for example one of my personal favorites, Solar Automated Mechanical Unit 31: (S.A.M.U.31 or Samuel). I knew I wanted a robot-like character, but I wanted to make sure there was a little more to Samuel than just being a robot. So I began brainstorming… “What would make a robot character interesting? Not to mention, what kind of interaction could the player be given to help out Samuel?” That’s how I started every time I made a new character, questions.

But to save you all of the borning details of me talking to myself in my room trying to brainstorm, I eventually settled on this idea: Samuel is a scientist from a barren planet, solely inhabited by machines. Water, food and vegetation doesn’t exist in this world, however, the planet is abundant in minerals, metals, and other materials necessary for machines. Their “species” was made as an experiment by another species to see if a group of non-organic sentient life can flourish on a planet without the necessary organic matter required for other living organisms. The denizens of this world build and manufacture their people based on the needs of society, with the people of the world being built to be solar powered, getting all of their energy from the sun. As a result, they have developed a religion based on the sun, as it is the thing that gives them life. Their homes and buildings lack roofs and are covered with windows for optimal sun absorption. The common citizens are unaware that they were originally made by another species. There’s a little more story on Samuel’s own backstory and motivation, but I’ll let you figure that out for yourself.

Since then I have come up with many more characters, worlds, their stories, and ideas this universe has to offer. This has been my first real time ever doing character and world building so while I was very worried at first, it has really paid off. I am super happy with all that I have created, and there is so much more for you to discover in our game itself, so I hope you enjoy! Thanks for reading!

-Hayden Huber
